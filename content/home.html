Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design

We design experiments and measurement strategies to reduce uncertainty and inform decisions, accounting for the costs of gathering information.

Scientific Machine Learning and Inverse Problems

We connect observations, physical models, and probabilistic inference to learn governing mechanisms and predict scientific systems.

Computational Uncertainty Quantification

We develop multifidelity estimators, surrogate models, and adaptive sampling methods for uncertainty analysis with expensive simulations.
