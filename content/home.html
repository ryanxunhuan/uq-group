Our Research

Explore the Themes

Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design

We design experiments and measurement strategies to reduce uncertainty and inform decisions, accounting for the costs of gathering information.

Scientific Machine Learning and Inverse Problems

We connect observations, physical models, and probabilistic inference to learn governing mechanisms and predict scientific systems.

Computational Uncertainty Quantification

We develop multifidelity estimators, surrogate models, and adaptive sampling methods for uncertainty analysis with expensive simulations.

Review article: Optimal experimental design: Formulations and computations · Acta Numerica, 2024.

Choosing Experiments and Deciding When to Stop

Adaptive experiments balance future learning against cost, including the decision to stop collecting data.

Learning Governing Equations from Noisy Observations

Bayesian inference and weak-form methods identify governing equations from noisy observations while quantifying uncertainty.

Learning Uncertain Fields with Fewer Expensive Simulations

Bifidelity surrogates and active learning combine inexpensive and detailed simulations to predict uncertain flow fields.
