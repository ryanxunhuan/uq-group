Inferring Battery Health from Partial Charging Data

A physics-informed model uses partial charging data to infer internal aging-related parameters. Those parameters help estimate battery health and connect measured voltage to the cell’s electrochemical behavior.

Published · eTransportation · 2026

The Scientific Question

A charging-voltage trace provides an accessible measurement of battery behavior. Interpreting it in terms of internal degradation requires a model that connects observable signals with the physical state of the cell.

Our Approach

We develop a parameterized physics-informed neural network for a single-particle electrochemical model. It represents internal behavior across selected aging-related parameters, which are inferred from tail-end charging profiles. A separate model combines the inferred parameters with voltage information to estimate state of health.

What This Work Shows

Tests on commercial pouch cells show that incorporating internal parameters improves health estimates and supports transfer across the charging profiles, health levels, and operating conditions studied.

Scope of the Study

The model omits electrolyte dynamics and is intended for moderate charging rates. Additional degradation mechanisms and demanding charging profiles require richer physics and further validation.

The Paper

X. Gu, X. Huan, Y. Ren, W. Zhou, W. Jiang and Z. Song (2026), Real-time physics-aware battery health monitoring from partial charging profiles via physics-informed neural networks, eTransportation 28, 100555.
