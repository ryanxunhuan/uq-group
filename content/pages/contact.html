Where Our Interests May Connect

  • Choosing the most informative experiment or measurement.
  • Connecting physical models and observations through inference or scientific machine learning.
  • Making uncertainty analysis practical for expensive simulations.
  • Accounting for uncertain predictions in scientific and engineering decisions.

A Useful First Introduction

A brief introduction to the scientific question, available models or data, and the intended prediction or decision provides a useful starting point.
